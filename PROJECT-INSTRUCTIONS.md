# Video edit – standing rules (paste into the claude.ai Project "Video edit" → Instructions)

I edit reels and ads (mostly Kapiva, 9:16, 25 fps) in Premiere Pro. Full rules are in the video-studio skill's house-style.md; these are the ones that always apply.

## How we work
- Frames first: show still frames / storyboard with 2–3 options for anything new, wait for my approval, then make the video preview, then build in Premiere.
- Approved frames are locked. Change only what my notes ask. Add nothing I didn't ask for (no extra lines, disclaimers, effects).
- With a script, take only the on-screen supers and wait until I name which hook/film to make.
- Never edit my sequences; duplicate and work on the copy. I save the Premiere project myself.
- Ask the export location once per project (Kapiva drafts go to `5. EXPORT/DRAFTS`).
- Previews always go in my Review Player with a link I can open.
- Don't use agents unless I say so.

## Look
- No colour grade. No fades on cuts. Never speed up B-roll; B-roll is full frame.
- Drop shadow on all text.
- No zoom in multi-cut stretches (e.g. the first 20 s of a hook). Slow pushes only on longer shots.
- Flash transitions with a swish SFX on big section changes. Energetic music, ducked under VO, long enough for the Body.
- No home-made illustrations; use real B-roll or AI B-roll (I generate it in Google Flow from your prompts).

## Text
- All supers, text and boxes are Premiere Pro text layers, one element per layer. After Effects only for graph/bar animation.
- Use the approved super typography family (Inter Tight / Montserrat caps, lime #C6F23A highlight box, red #E8262C, Kapiva green #1E5B2E tag, white boxes). Pick type and tone per video; don't reuse one look everywhere.
- "Bold and slimmer" / "bold and light" = two lines, all caps, white, centred: top line Jost Bold, bottom line Jost Light, tight leading, soft shadow. Lime top line or left-aligned are allowed variants.

## Kapiva
- Brand: brand-kapiva skill. Health claims: "helps manage / supports / may help", never "cures".
- Copy product names exactly from the label.
