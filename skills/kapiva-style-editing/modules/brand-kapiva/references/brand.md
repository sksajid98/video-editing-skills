# Brand: Kapiva (client)

Status: SET 2026-10-31. Source: kapiva.in live CSS (color frequency + `--cr-primary` variables), font stacks,
headings, and the official logo file (~/Downloads/kapiva-logo-ai.png). Replace with the official brand book when
the user provides it.

Positioning: "Modern Ayurvedic nutrition". Natural ingredients, everyday wellness, science-backed Ayurveda.

## Palette
| Role | Name | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|---|
| Primary | Olive | #506000 | Headings, key blocks, buttons, title cards (white text on it) |
| Primary light | Leaf Green | #80A03C | Highlights, icons, charts, progress bars. Not for small text on white |
| Primary dark | Deep Moss | #59702F | Hover/pressed, gradients from Olive |
| Text | Ink | #1E1E1E | Body text, captions stroke |
| Background | White | #FFFFFF | Default background |
| Background soft | Sage | #EDEFE5 | Cards, lower-third plates, callouts |
| Background soft 2 | Sage Deep | #E6EAD8 | Alternating sections |
| Accent | Terracotta | #C97B5A | One highlight per piece (price, key word, badge) |
| Alert | Rust | #C74C17 | Offers, sale tags, warnings only |
| Neutral | Grey | #666666 | Secondary text, fine print |

Measured contrast (WCAG): White on Olive 6.97:1 ✓ · Ink on Sage 14.35:1 ✓ · Ink on Terracotta 5.13:1 ✓ ·
Leaf on White 3.0:1 ✗ (shapes / large display only) · White on Terracotta 3.25:1 ✗ (use Ink text on Terracotta).

## Typography
- Heading: **Jost** (SemiBold 600 / Bold 700), all-caps allowed for short labels with +40 tracking (echoes the
  wordmark). Fallback: Nunito, then Arial.
- Body: **Jost** Regular 400 / Medium 500. Fallback: Nunito, Arial.
- Both are free Google Fonts. Install Jost locally before any AE/Premiere work.

## Logo
- File: `assets/kapiva-logo.png`. 860×280, solid black badge with a white geometric wordmark and wave notches on
  the left and right edges (the notches are transparent).
- Works on white, sage or light footage. On dark or olive backgrounds, keep the badge with a clear-space margin.
- Clear space: at least the height of the "K" on all sides. Minimum width: 120 px (1080p video) or 25 mm (print).
- Don't recolor, outline, stretch, rotate or add effects. Don't recreate the wordmark in a font.

## Voice
- Tone words: modern, trustworthy, warm, natural, simple.
- Say: "modern Ayurveda", "natural ingredients", "helps manage", "supports", "daily wellness", benefit-first lines.
- Avoid: "cure", "treat", "miracle", "guaranteed", "100% results", fear-based or medical-replacement claims, heavy jargon.
- Disclaimer when a condition is mentioned: "Consult your doctor before use." (or the client's exact wording).
- CTA: "Shop now on kapiva.in". Confirm with the client per campaign.

## Video look
- Captions: Jost Bold, White, Ink 3–4 px stroke or soft shadow, bottom-safe area. One highlight word in Leaf
  Green. Sentence case.
- Lower thirds: Sage plate with an Olive 6 px left bar, name in Jost SemiBold Ink, title in Jost Regular Grey.
- Title cards: Olive full-frame, White Jost Bold, optional thin Leaf underline.
- End card: White/Sage, logo centered, product name in Olive, CTA line, disclaimer in Grey small.
- Motion feel: smooth and calm. Ease-out slides and gentle scale-ins (8–12 frames). No glitch, shake or neon.
- Transitions: straight cuts, plus soft cross-dissolves for mood shifts only.
