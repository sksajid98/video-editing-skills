# Method source and prompts

Source: Ikjot Juneja, "3 Claude Prompts That Find Your Next Viral Video (Before You Write It)",
ikjotjuneja.substack.com, 29 Oct 2026. The idea: study what already won, add what only you know, then try to
break it before filming. The article runs step 1 on the author's Creatorberry tool; this skill replaces that
with web research plus `scripts/outlier_score.py`. Prompts below are rewritten in this skill's own words.

Read this when you want the exact wording for a step, or the user asks where the method comes from.

## Step 1 prompt (find)
"Find the 10 strongest outlier videos in <niche> on <platform> from the last 30 days. An outlier is a video
far above its own channel's normal views, not the most-viewed video overall. Group them by hook type, topic
and format, then name the 3 patterns that repeat."

## Step 2 prompt (interview)
"Using those 3 patterns, interview me about my experience in <niche>. One question at a time, wait for my
answer. Keep going until you can give me 3 ideas that follow a proven pattern and that only I could make."

## Step 3 prompt (try to kill it)
"Here is my idea: <idea>. Be harsh. 1) What's the weakest part? 2) What pushback will the comments give?
3) Write 10 hooks, each from a different angle."

## Hook types (use these labels when grouping)
Shock number · bold or contrarian claim · question with stakes · "I tried X" / experiment · mistake or warning
("stop doing X") · before → after · list ("3 things") · story with a named person · myth vs fact ·
insider secret · trend reaction / "this just happened" · comparison (X vs Y).

## Formats (use these labels)
Talking head · voiceover + B-roll · skit / acting · tutorial or screen recording · street interview ·
green-screen reaction · text-on-screen only · product demo / unboxing · day-in-the-life · duet / stitch.
